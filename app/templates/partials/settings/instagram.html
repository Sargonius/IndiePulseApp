Setup (needs Meta App Review)

  1. The IG account must be Business/Creator and linked to a Facebook Page (Facebook section above).
  2. The instagram_business_content_publish permission goes through Meta App Review (~2–4 weeks).
  3. Get the IG Business Account ID: Graph API Explorer → GET /{page-id}?fields=instagram_business_account.
  4. Use the linked page’s Page Access Token as the token.
Until the review passes, Reels go to the export folder (upload them manually from your phone). Once approved — full auto-posting.
