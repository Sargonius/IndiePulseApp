How updates work

IndiePulse looks for a new release on GitHub 30 seconds after it starts and every 6 hours. Installing downloads the release, checks its checksum, waits until nothing is publishing, then restarts IndiePulse on the new version. If the new version doesn't come up, the previous one is put back by itself. Your settings, posts and media are never touched (they live in the data folder).
