How it works

  1. Write the post in the first language; Translate missing in the post form fills the others (the AI from “AI & translation”, else DeepL / free translators).
  2. Each destination gets the text in its language. No text in it: the first language’s, else any — unless “only its language” is ticked, then nothing goes there.
  3. Telegram, Discord, Facebook and Reddit can have several (a channel per language); the others are one account each, so you pick their language.
  4. Destinations made from the fields below (Telegram chat ID, Facebook pages, Discord webhooks, subreddit) stay linked to them: change either, both change.
  5. Destinations of a module that is off in 🧩 Modules are hidden and nothing is sent to them.
