Video editor

Opens from the video tile in “📷 Post” and on “🎬 Video”: format (original / 16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1), crop, trim, music from these folders, on-screen text.

A video over a network’s limit is re-compressed to fit when publishing (Discord, Telegram 50 MB, Bluesky 100 MB…). If even that doesn’t help, the post fails with an error instead of going out without the video. The editor shows in advance what will happen on each network.
