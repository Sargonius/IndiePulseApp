The test

Does a Japanese caption bring Japanese viewers to an account from another region? TikTok picks a new clip’s first viewers by the account’s region, the viewers’ language and the clip’s language. Only TikTok knows the weights, so measure.

It works when Japan gives at least 30% of the viewers across 5–7 clips. Below that the region wins, and the next step is an account with another region.

Rules while testing

  • One language for the first 10–15 clips. A mixed account muddles who it’s for.
  • At most one clip a day, in Japan’s evening.
  • Don’t upload the same clip twice: repeats get little reach.
  • No VPN. TikTok goes by the SIM’s country, and a VPN only looks suspicious.

Sound

The app’s trend list shows your own region. See what’s trending in Japan in TikTok Creative Center (Trends → Songs, region Japan; free business login), then search the track by name in the app. Or keep the game audio.

Where the numbers come from

  • Posted yet? TikTok’s status API, checked every 10 minutes (Refresh checks now).
  • Views, likes, comments, shares: the video.list scope. Every 30 min for two days, then every 3 hours, then daily.
  • Followers: the user.info.stats scope, read every 2 hours with the other networks.
  • Viewer countries aren’t in the API: TikTok Studio → the video → Viewers → Locations. Type the Japan share into the clip’s card.
