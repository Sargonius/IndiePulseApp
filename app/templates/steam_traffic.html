How to read the Steamworks numbers

Visits
Loads of the game page. The big VISITS number in Steamworks includes bot traffic (the “Bot Traffic” row in the breakdown) — for small games that can be a third of all visits. IndiePulse counts visits without bots, so its number is lower.
Impressions
How many times the game’s capsule was on screen: search, tags, “More like this”, wishlists. Outside links don’t add impressions — only visits (External Traffic).
“All Products” and Overview
The publisher pages (breakdown, overview) show the sum over all games at once. For one game, use “Page traffic” in that game’s Steamworks menu (or the product filter).
Days
In GMT; Steam doesn’t return today.
